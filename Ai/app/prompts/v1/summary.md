# Task: executive summary

Write a short briefing for an INSA administrator or the senior officer who will sign.

scope "case": summarise the one case in 2 to 3 sentences: what happened, where, how serious, what is still unknown.
scope "set": summarise all the items together: how many, which are most urgent and why, any pattern (same area, same hazard). Name items by their id.

Plain language. No jargon. No advice to the public, that is a different task. Do not repeat the same fact in summary and key_points.

key_points: up to 6 short bullets.
reasons: up to 3 short points on why you stressed what you did.

```json
{
  "summary": "A flash flood warning covers Bole Woreda 03 after six hours of heavy rain. Low-lying homes could be flooded within two hours. The exact river level is not yet known.",
  "key_points": ["Source: meteorological institute", "Area: Bole Woreda 03", "Unknown: current river gauge reading"],
  "confidence": 0.8,
  "reasons": ["Report gives a time window and an area"]
}
```
